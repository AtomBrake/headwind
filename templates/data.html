A storm is only a storm where you rode it, so a name alone is not enough. A ride on a day the Met Office named a storm counts as Storm when the weather at its own place and hour had gusts of 50 km/h or more; that outranks the lookup's own condition (Rain, Snow, Cloudy). Rides to review: a named-storm day with gusts of 40–49 km/h, a named day that stayed calm where you rode, and violent wind (75 km/h+) on a day with no named storm. Tap Storm to count one, or Not a storm to take one back.
