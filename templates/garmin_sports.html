Check activity types with Garmin

An older Garmin sync saved every activity type it didn't know as a ride, so swims, surf sessions and some runs ended up in your rides, counted in your ride totals and flagged as rides that look wrong. Runs, walks and hikes were also saved as rides. It also saved mountain bike and gravel rides as plain rides; given their own kind back, they get your default bike for that kind (Gear page). So did syncs before Headwind knew Garmin's e-bike types: those rides were saved as plain rides. And HIIT, strength and yoga sessions saved before Headwind had those sports were saved as Other. This asks Garmin for the real type of each Garmin ride and lists the ones that are wrong. Nothing changes until you apply it. Headwind also lists them by itself when it starts, from the type Garmin gave each activity the last time it was synced or checked.
