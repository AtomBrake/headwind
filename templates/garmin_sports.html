Check activity types with Garmin

An older Garmin sync saved every activity type it didn't know as a ride, so swims, surf sessions and some runs ended up in your rides, counted in your ride totals and flagged as rides that look wrong. Runs, walks and hikes were also kept with the rides instead of in Workouts. This asks Garmin for the real type of each Garmin ride and lists the ones that are wrong.
