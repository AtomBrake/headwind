Headwind

A self-hosted, private training and health app for every sport. It started as a cycling app, and rides are still its deepest part, but runs, walks, hikes, swims, ski days, paddles, strength sessions and the rest are activities in their own right, and a distance only ever adds up with others of its kind. Your data stays on your own hardware, in one database you can copy and back up. Activities come from Garmin Connect, the Headwind iPhone and Apple Watch app, and FIT, GPX, TCX or Strava-export imports.

Activities
  • Every sport: road, mountain bike, gravel, e-bike and indoor rides; runs, walks and hikes; pool and open-water swims; ski days; paddles, surfs, kitesurfs and sails; strength, yoga and HIIT
  • Garmin sync: activities of every type and daily recovery data, with a connect flow in the browser that handles two-factor sign-in
  • iPhone and Apple Watch app: records any sport, with Bluetooth sensors, and uploads it; can share a live tracking link while you record
  • File import: .fit, .gpx, .tcx, or a zipped Strava data export, with live progress
  • Duplicates: the same activity from Garmin, Strava, a file and the phone is kept once, the better recording winning
  • GPX export of any activity with a GPS track, from its page
  • Several riders, each with their own profile, stats, best times, segment PRs and trophy case
Activity pages
  • A map with satellite, topographic and cycling map types, distance markers and wind arrows
  • Speed or pace, elevation, heart rate, power and cadence charts, linked to the map
  • Pages that fit the sport: pace and splits on foot, pace per 100 m and SWOLF for swims, each run of a ski day, no climb on the water
  • Laps from your device, segment efforts and PRs, and the other times you've done the same route
  • Zwift rides and runs drawn on their own world's map
  • AI coaching notes on a ride, on demand or as each new ride arrives
Best times and records
  • Rides: fastest 5, 10, 20, 30, 50, 75 and 100 miles (5 to 160 km with metric units), the fastest stretch inside any one ride
  • On foot: 1 km, 1 mile, 3 km, 5 km, 10 km, 15 km, half marathon and marathon
  • In the water: 100 m to 3.8 km
  • Indoor rides and runs have tables of their own and never count towards your outdoor times
  • Records by sport: longest, longest time, most climbing and fastest, all time and by year
  • Year and month filters; the AI coach is told about any record an activity set
Trophy case
  • Badges for riding (ride count, distance, elevation, epic rides, speed, climbing, weather, segments), running, walking and hiking, swimming, segments on foot, other sports and all sports together
  • Each badge links to the activity that earned it
  • Weather badges for cold, heat, rain, storms and headwinds, with Record Low and Record Gale linking to your coldest and windiest rides
  • Indoor rides and runs count towards the badges for counts and time, not distance, climbing, speed or weather
  • Unearned badges are shown faded, as goals
Analytics and goals
  • Speed or pace over time with a rolling average, monthly distance, year on year, an activity calendar
  • Activity length distribution and activities by day of week, for one sport or several
  • Weather scatter charts: speed against temperature and wind, average speed by condition; each dot links to its activity
  • Goals: distance, climbing, time or number of activities for a sport, each week, month or year
  • Weeks and months as the past 7 and 28 days, or calendar ones, as each rider chooses
Segments
  • Define a segment on any activity's map: click the start, click the end, name it
  • Every past activity of its kind is scanned for efforts in the background
  • Ride segments and on-foot segments; rides only match ride segments, runs, walks and hikes only on-foot ones
  • PRs, leaderboards (bike and e-bike efforts apart or together), effort history, trend charts and a difficulty rating
Weather
  • Weather at the start of every outdoor activity from Open-Meteo (free, no API key)
  • Temperature, wind speed, direction and gusts, humidity, rain and conditions
  • Headwind, tailwind or crosswind, worked out from the route's bearing
  • Older activities are filled in a few at each sync, or all at once from the Tasks page
AI coaching
  • Coaching notes on your rides from OpenAI's GPT models, Claude, or a local Ollama model, with your own key
  • The prompt includes the ride's stats, weather, same-route comparisons, similar past activities, segment efforts, your other training and recovery
  • Ten personalities, from encouraging to blunt, and your own coaching goals
  • Written as each new ride arrives, or on demand for older ones
Recovery and health
  • Resting heart rate, HRV, sleep, steps, stress and body battery from Garmin Connect, or from Apple Health through the iPhone app
  • 30, 60 and 90-day charts for each
  • Food diary with barcode scanning, hydration, and a weight trend with calorie goals that count your training
  • Recovery on a ride's date is part of the AI coaching prompt
Gear
  • Bikes, shoes and other kit, with a default for each kind of activity
  • Wear on chains, tyres, cassettes and other parts, worked out from your activities, with a service log
  • Alerts when a part or a pair of shoes is due, on the dashboard and through Home Assistant's companion app
Home Assistant
  • MQTT sensors found by Home Assistant itself: activities and training time of every sport, this week's distance by kind (rides, on foot, swims), the latest activity, ride totals (distance, climbing, Everests, laps of the Earth) and the last ride, badges, recovery, nutrition, hydration and weight
  • A Running tab: best times, pace, totals, recent runs and the latest route
  • Live sensors while the phone records: status, speed, distance, time and elevation
  • Published when something changes, with a check every 5 minutes
  • Notifications through the Home Assistant companion app when a new activity arrives or gear is due, linking to its page
Heatmap and friends
  • A GPS heatmap of every sport across your whole history, with a date range and sport filter
  • HD export at 3440×1440
  • Link your Headwind to a friend's to share activities, segments, the heatmap and challenges, with no central server
Stack
  • Backend: Python, Flask and SQLite
  • Frontend: plain JavaScript, Chart.js and Leaflet
  • Data sources: Garmin Connect, Open-Meteo, Open Food Facts, the Headwind iPhone and Apple Watch app
  • Integrations: Home Assistant over MQTT
